The first component library, built before there was a platform team to own one.
Forty-odd controls, no theming layer, and an API shaped by whichever product
team needed something first.

It worked, and it was the right thing to build at the time, and almost every
architectural decision in it turned out to be wrong at scale — most of all the
absence of a theming layer, which meant colour values were literals in a
hundred components and stayed that way for years.

Archived and superseded. Kept public because it is a useful artefact: a
straight line from "make it work" to the constraints that shaped everything
after it.
